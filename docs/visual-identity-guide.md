# Visual Identity Guide

## Visual direction

- Premium atmospheric background, no plain white scaffolding.
- Primary surfaces are translucent and layered.
- Controlled saturation with natural material realism.
- Calm transitions, no aggressive contrast jumps.

## Typography

- Primary display: a high-contrast serif for headers.
- Body: a humane sans with strong readability.
- Always pair with generous line-height and controlled tracking.

## Color system

- Deep neutrals as base.
- Material-adaptive accent tokens for controls and chips.
- Elevated surfaces: glass-like depth with light blur.
- Contrast-safe text over gradients by always testing WCAG AA.

## Interaction language

- Subtle ink-in style focus states.
- Soft card shadows and edge glow.
- Confirmation chips should visually out-rank destructive actions.

## Media standard

- Product photos: editorial-grade realism.
- AI renders: 16:9 and 4:3 support with consistent depth cues.
- Motion imagery: no jump-cut montage; use dissolve and drift.
