# Product Vision: Signature Bathroom Journey

## Vision statement

Build an AI-guided, cinematic renovation companion that helps local customers design and finalize bathroom projects with the confidence of a professional designer and renovation expert.

## What we are building

- A guided renovation journey for one domain only: bathrooms.
- A dual-flow model:
  - **Existing bathroom**: photo-led reconstruction and controlled modernization.
  - **New bathroom**: design-first exploration with palette/material discovery and style curation.
- A trust-first interface that reduces cognitive load and encourages decision confidence.

## What this is not

- No webshop.
- No generic product configurator.
- No kitchen, house, or HVAC workflows.

## Business outcome

- Increase qualified leads from high-intent renovation clients.
- Reduce pre-sales support friction with guided scope decisions.
- Improve conversion from first consult to design approval.
- Preserve premium brand perception through premium interaction quality.

## Success signal

- A customer can produce a reviewable room concept in a calm, guided flow with explicit consent at every AI-assisted step.
- Sales team receives a complete, interpretable context package:
  - room measurements
  - fixture inventory confidence map
  - selected styles/materials
  - next-step action recommendations
